# Cérebro Galáctico — plugin Obsidian (prévia)

Mode: Experience. Replaces the archived "Cérebro em raio X" world (arquivo/plugin-cerebro-raio-x, knowledge in conhecimento/cerebro-em-raio-x.md). Same owner, product truth, data, events, limits (read-only, offline, integrated GPU ≥30 fps with 1,300 notes, pause when hidden, reduced effects, prefers-reduced-motion, must sit beside the Cerebro Obsidian theme without replacing it). Owner choices 2026-10-10: navigation = flight through the galaxy; dust grows global + local with synapses; thunder on all four events; name "Cérebro Galáctico".

## Direction contract

THESIS: the vault is a spiral galaxy that a pilot flies through. Notes are stars, and every synapse deposits stardust, so the galaxy literally thickens as knowledge links up. It refuses the flat node graph and the generic purple-glow WebGL galaxy loop.

OWN-WORLD: Hubble SHO palette from the owner's plates. Ground is the theme's #070b16. Rust and orange gas (#8c3b22, #e0733a) sit on the outer arms, gold (#f0b44c) on the dense lanes, and teal and cavity blue (#3cc3d0, #2a4fb8) in the hollow around a warm-white core (#fff1d6). Dark dust lanes are cut by absorption. Stars are white tinted by type, hubs carry 4-point diffraction spikes, and firing uses the theme type tokens. HUD in Manrope, hairline only.

STORY: open in the morning to a galaxy turning slowly. Scrolling flies into it through layered dust toward the pointer. A click flies to a star and opens the note. Work happens as weather: opening sends light along the lanes, saving throws lightning, a capture raises a storm from the core, and a new note condenses from dust.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the galaxy at about 40° tilt (28° flattened the spiral in the first render), its disk filling about 80% of the width. The warm core sits slightly above centre, inside its teal cavity. Top left: "Cérebro Galáctico" with 1.114 estrelas · 5.955 sinapses. Top right: search (Ctrl+K) and filters. Bottom: nothing but sky (the owner removed the signal strip on 2026-10-10); the preview-only simulation buttons sit in the bottom-right corner. Hover shows the star's name.

FORM: owner's pick (flight-through nebula, reference video 1) over the dealt orienteering-map direction. The world is pinned by the owner's references (SHO nebula plate, nebula flight, cosmic vortex). Seed key db2a0df3. Signature moment: saving a note throws branching lightning from its star to its linked stars, and the flash lights the surrounding dust from inside, like lightning in a cloud.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
