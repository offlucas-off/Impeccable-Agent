## Stack

## Users

One user: the owner of the Cérebro vault (Lucas). He opens it on the PC during the weekly review (Monday, 09:00), when the `cerebro` agent brings the batch approval list. In that moment his job is to decide what to approve, what to clear first, and which project is bleeding. Secondary readers are the agents themselves (`cerebro`, MAESTRO, project chats), which keep reading `metricas.md`, not the panel.

## Product Purpose

The Cérebro is a personal Obsidian vault that turns every mistake an AI agent makes into a rule, so the mistake does not happen again ("Erro já cometido não se repete"). Its metrics file reports the health of that loop: the approval queue, the approved items still waiting for application, rules that keep recurring, open incidents per project, and the token cost of the rules. Success: the owner leaves the weekly review knowing what to approve and what is late, without reading the raw Markdown.

## Positioning

The vault's own mechanism is the **blindagem ladder**: 0 incident → 1 rule in guardrails → 2 step in a skill/checklist → 3 automated check → 4 block (a hook or tool refuses). Every recurrence climbs one step. The panel's unique truth is whether that ladder is keeping up with reality: rules that recur while still low on the ladder are the core risk.

## Operating Context

- Source of truth: `90_SISTEMA/metricas.md` (regenerated by script, never edited by hand) and `90_SISTEMA/metricas-historico.csv` (one row per day since 2026-10-04).
- Writer chain: `cerebro` consolidates → `gerar_guardrails` → `metricas` → `validar` → commit. Single writer per file (RG-GLOBAL-008).
- Review protocol (BRAIN-VAULT.md): in the review, clear the **ATRASADAS** and the **"alta reincidente"** items first.
- Approvals are in batch, A/B with the Cérebro's recommendation (RG-GLOBAL-035); the acceptance rate of recommendations is tracked.
- Projects: GLOBAL, NWL, CORTES-GAMER, MOZI, VIDEO, TESTEDISC (plus CENTRAL, AFILIADO-ML without metrics yet).

## Product Principles

1. Order of attention follows the review protocol: late first, then high-severity recurrences, then the approval queue.
2. Show the ladder, not just counts: a rule's step (n1–n4) next to its recurrences is the signal.
3. Numbers, short, with what failed (RG-GLOBAL-019).
4. Never compete with the writer chain: the panel is a view of `metricas.md`, never a second source of truth.
